1. Overview & Commitment

Market-OS ("Platform", "we", "our") is dedicated to uncompromising security and privacy for institutional and proprietary trading participants. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, encrypt, and process information when you use our services or apply for our waitlist.

2. Information We Collect

  • Waitlist Information: Full name, professional email address, primary broker platform, and qualitative trading interest.
  • Account Credentials: Email, salted and hashed passwords (bcrypt standard).
  • Cloud Broker Connections: MetaTrader 5 server addresses, account logins, and encrypted investor/trade credentials provided exclusively for automated execution.
  • Execution Telemetry: Real-time fill prices, slippage metrics, order latency, and trade journal outcomes.

3. Zero-Knowledge Credential Security

All broker passwords and API tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 / Fernet symmetric cryptographic ciphers with server-side key derivation. Broker credentials are never stored in plaintext and are only decrypted in isolated worker sub-processes strictly for authenticated order dispatch.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Broker Access

Market-OS does never sell, rent, or monetize your trading data or personally identifiable information. Your trading activity is never shared with third-party liquidity providers or market makers.

5. GDPR & Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR and international privacy statutes, you possess the right to:

  • Request a complete export of your personal trading telemetry and journal history.
  • Revoke broker account authorization instantly with a single click.
  • Request permanent purging and erasure of all associated broker credentials and account records.

6. Contact & Data Officer

To request data erasure or query our privacy practices, contact the security team directly at security@market-os.io.
